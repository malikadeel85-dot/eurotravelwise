Best European Cities for a Budget Trip in 2026

Discover affordable European destinations, practical travel ideas, activities and planning tips for your next trip.

🇭🇺
BUDAPEST

1. Budapest, Hungary

Budapest is one of the best-value cities in Europe. Visitors can enjoy beautiful architecture, thermal baths, river views and traditional Hungarian food at reasonable prices.

Best things to do:

- Visit the Hungarian Parliament area
- Walk along the Danube River
- Explore Buda Castle
- Enjoy a traditional Hungarian meal
- Relax in a thermal bath

PRAGUE

2. Prague, Czech Republic

Prague is famous for its historic streets, charming bridges and beautiful old town. Many of its best attractions can be explored on foot.

Best things to do:

- Visit Prague Old Town Square
- Walk across Charles Bridge
- Explore Prague Castle
- Enjoy the city's cafés
- Walk through the historic streets
