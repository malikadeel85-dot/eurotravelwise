Best Hotels in Paris 2026: Areas, Prices & Where to Stay

Choose the right Paris neighborhood for your trip with this practical guide to location, atmosphere, transport, accommodation styles and typical budgets.

Paris has accommodation across every budget and travel style, but the neighborhood you choose can make a major difference to your daily travel time. The official Paris tourism office describes the city's arrondissements as having distinct characters, price ranges and transport access.

Planning tip: For a first visit, compare the hotel location with the Metro before booking. A hotel a little farther from a landmark can be convenient if it is close to a useful Metro line.

1. Louvre and the 1st Arrondissement

The 1st arrondissement puts you close to the Louvre, Tuileries Garden, Rue de Rivoli and the Châtelet-Les Halles transport hub. Paris je t'aime describes it as the very heart of the city and notes that many attractions are within walking distance.

Hotels here range from luxury properties to higher-end aparthotels. The trade-off is that central locations generally command higher rates.

2. The Marais and Notre-Dame Area

The 2nd, 3rd and 4th arrondissements include the Marais and areas around Notre-Dame. Paris je t'aime characterizes this part of Paris by its historic streets, boutiques, galleries, restaurants and strong cultural atmosphere.

This area can work well for travelers who want to combine sightseeing with cafés, shopping and evening activities. Metro access is also strong, including Lines 1, 4 and 11.

3. Latin Quarter — 5th Arrondissement

The Latin Quarter is a central option with a lively atmosphere and access to major sights on the Left Bank. It can be a practical choice for travelers who want central accommodation without focusing exclusively on luxury hotels.

4. Saint-Germain-des-Prés — 6th Arrondissement

The 6th arrondissement is associated with classic Paris cafés, culture and elegant streets. It is particularly suited to visitors who want to spend time exploring the Left Bank on foot while remaining close to central attractions.

5. Eiffel Tower and the 7th Arrondissement

The 7th arrondissement covers the Eiffel Tower and Invalides area. Staying here can be convenient when the Eiffel Tower is a major part of your itinerary. Hotel prices vary considerably depending on the exact street, hotel category and views.

6. Opera and the 9th Arrondissement

The 9th arrondissement around Opera and the Grands Boulevards offers central access, shopping and connections to other parts of Paris. It is worth comparing hotels near Metro stations rather than choosing solely by distance from a landmark.

7. Bastille and the 11th Arrondissement

The 11th arrondissement around Bastille and République has a lively restaurant and nightlife scene. Paris je t'aime lists Bastille among areas where travelers can find more affordable accommodation compared with some of the most prestigious central districts.

8. Montmartre and the 18th Arrondissement

Montmartre is one of Paris's best-known neighborhoods, centered around the hill and Sacré-Cœur. It offers a distinctive artistic and romantic atmosphere. When booking here, check the walking route to the nearest Metro station because some streets are steep.

9. Budget-Friendly Areas

For travelers watching accommodation costs, Paris je t'aime identifies areas such as Belleville in the 20th arrondissement and the Canal Saint-Martin area as options with strong Metro connections and a more local atmosphere. Bastille and parts of the 10th can also provide alternatives to the most expensive central areas.

10. Family and Longer-Stay Options

Families and longer-stay visitors may want to consider aparthotels. These properties can provide kitchen facilities and more living space. Paris je t'aime notes that aparthotels are particularly common in areas such as the 15th arrondissement and around La Défense.

When Should You Book a Paris Hotel?

  • For popular travel periods, compare hotels several months ahead rather than waiting until the last minute.
  • Check whether your dates overlap with major events, trade fairs or unusually busy periods.
  • Compare the total price, including taxes and any mandatory charges.
  • Check recent guest reviews and the hotel's cancellation conditions.
  • Confirm the walking distance to the nearest Metro station.

How to Choose the Right Paris Hotel

  1. Choose your sightseeing priorities: Decide whether the Louvre, Eiffel Tower, Montmartre, Left Bank or another area will dominate your itinerary.
  2. Check transport: A convenient Metro connection can matter more than being a few hundred meters closer to a landmark.
  3. Set your nightly budget: Compare several neighborhoods instead of searching only in the city center.
  4. Check room details: Paris rooms can be compact, so confirm room size, elevator access and air conditioning when important to you.
  5. Read recent reviews: Focus on comments about cleanliness, noise, staff and the current condition of the property.
